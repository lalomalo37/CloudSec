RJ (Registered Jack)-45: Your typical Ethernet cable that connects to an RJ Port.

Ethernet: 
- Is a collection of network protocols/standards
- For now, we will focus on types of cabling as defined by Ethernet standards

Bit:
- 0
- 1

Byte:
- 8 Bits = 1 Byte
- Speed is measured in bits per seconds

1 kilobit (kb) - 1,000 bits
1 megabig (Mb) - 1,000,000 bits
1 gigabit (Gb) - 1,000,000,000 bits
1 terabit (Tb) 10^12 - 1,000,000,000,000 bits
petabit 10^15
exabit 10^18
zettabit 10^21
yottabit 10^24

Ethernet standards:
Defined in the IEEE 802.3 standard in 1983 (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)

UTP Cables:
- Unshielded, Twisted, Pair Cables.
- Protects against EMI (Electromagnetic Interference)
- 10BASE-T and 100BASE- T = 2 pairs (4 wires)
- 1000BASE-T and 10GBASE-t = 4 pairs (8 wires)
